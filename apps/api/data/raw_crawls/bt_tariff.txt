Black Thunder Water Theme Park Tariff & Charges 

The prices given below are effective from 1st Mar 2026 

Park Entry Charges 

Adult Rs. 1090/- 

Child (85cms to 140cms) Rs. 850- 

College Student Rs. 890/- 

School Student Rs. 790/- 

Sr. citizen/Defence Rs. 850/- 

Special Combo Ticket 
(Park Entry & Adventure Activities) Rs. 1,450/- 

Combo Ticket: Adventure  Activities Rs. 550/- 

*Child ticket-for kids within a height of 85cm and 140cm 
*Special Combo Ticket and Combo Adventure Activities (Minimum height: 145 cm | Maximum weight: 85 kg) 
*A minimum of 20 students from the same School or College are eligible for concession if visiting park with valid bonafide letter. 
*One staff (with a valid institutional ID card) entry is complimentary for every 15 students 
*Food can be arranged for general/corporate groups, school and college student groups. 
*Senior Citizens (60 years & above)/Defence personnel should show their identity proof to avail the ticket.
