DINING 

Note: Outside Foods are strictly Not allowed inside our MGM Dizzee world. 

Experience delightful dining at MGM Dizzee World with a range of culinary options to satisfy every palate. Enjoy vegetarian delights at Surfin Food Court or savour non-vegetarian treats at Dizzee Den. Whether it's lunch or evening snacks, our dining options add flavour to your fun-filled day at the park!

SURFIN 

Surfin Food Court in Chennai is a paradise for vegetarian food lovers. Indulge in a variety of delicious vegetarian dishes, carefully crafted to tantalise your taste buds. From flavourful Indian classics to international favourites, Surfin Food Court offers a diverse menu to satisfy every craving. 

DIZZEE DEN 

Our restaurant in Chennai, Dizzee Den is a haven for meat lovers. Offering a delectable range of non-vegetarian dishes, our eatery promises a culinary experience like no other. From savoury starters to sumptuous main courses, Dizzee Den is sure to delight your taste buds.
