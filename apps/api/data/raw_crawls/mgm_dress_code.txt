Dress Code at MGM Dizzee World 

Welcome to MGM Dizzee World, Chennai's best amusement park where fun and excitement await you! 

To ensure a safe, comfortable, and enjoyable experience for all our guests, we kindly request that visitors adhere to the following dress code guidelines: 

General Guidelines 

1. Appropriate Attire: All guests are required to wear appropriate attire at all times. This includes shirts, t-shirts, jeans, tracks, three-fourths, shorts or pants, and proper footwear. 

2. Footwear: Comfortable shoes are recommended for a day at the park. Flip-flops, sandals, and slippers are permitted, but guests should be cautious on rides where secure footwear is necessary. 

3. Swimwear: Guests planning to enjoy our water attractions must wear proper swimwear. Please refrain from wearing denim or clothing with metal buttons or zippers on water rides. Swimwear is also available at reasonable prices in the park. 

4. Prohibited Clothing: Clothing with offensive language or graphics, excessively torn garments, and see-through attire are not permitted. We reserve the right to deny entry to guests wearing inappropriate attire. 

Specific Guidelines for Rides 

1. Secure Clothing: For safety reasons, loose clothing, scarves, and accessories that can easily get caught in ride mechanisms are discouraged. Secure all loose items before boarding rides. 

2. Water Rides: Swimwear is mandatory on water rides. Dry clothes must be worn once leaving the water attractions to ensure the comfort of all guests. 

3. Safety Gear: On certain rides, guests may be required to wear additional safety gear. Please comply with all safety instructions provided by our staff. 

Tips for a Comfortable Visit 

1 . Weather-Appropriate Clothing: Chennai’s weather can be warm and humid. Light, breathable fabrics are recommended. Don’t forget your sunglasses, hats, and sunscreen! 

2. Change of Clothes: Bringing a change of clothes can enhance your comfort, especially if you plan to enjoy both dry and water attractions. 

3. Storage: Lockers are available for rent to store your personal belongings safely while you enjoy the park’s attractions. 

Thank you for your cooperation in helping us maintain a safe and family-friendly environment. We hope you have a fantastic time at MGM Dizzee World! 

For any queries or further information, please feel free to contact us. 

Note: MGM Dizzee World reserves the right to modify the dress code guidelines at any time without prior notice. Please re-check our website for the most up-to-date information on dress codes, before your visit.
