Terms & Conditions 

Black Thunder-Water Theme Park welcomes you to review our terms and conditions. Please find below the guidelines to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience: 

A minimum of 20 students from the same School or College are eligible for concession if visiting park with valid bonafide letter. 

One staff (with a valid institutional ID card) entry is complimentary for every 15 students 

Food can be arranged for general/corporate groups, school and college student groups. 

Senior Citizens (60 years & above)/Defence personnel should show their identity proof to avail the ticket. 

Child ticket-for kids within a height of 85cm and 140cm. If the height of the kids are found to be above 140cm during the entry verification, the guest would need to upgrade the ticket to an adult ticket paying the differential amount at the park ticket counter. 

Only one time entry is permitted per ticket and no subsequent entry would be permitted due to security reasons even in the case the guest needs a re-entry during the day of visit. 

Black Thunder Theme Park (P) Ltd, reserves the right to cancel tickets if they are found to be misused or misappropriated. 

To ensure the safety of our guests, height and weight restrictions are applicable for certain rides. 

Follow park guidelines to ensure a fun and secure experience.
