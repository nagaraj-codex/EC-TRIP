FAQs About Black Thunder - Water Theme Park 

Find answers to the most common questions about visiting Black Thunder Water Theme Park in Mettupalayam, Coimbatore. We are covering everything from tickets and rides to dining, accommodation at AVANA Resort, weddings, events, and how to reach the park. 

About Black Thunder Water Theme Park 

What is Black Thunder Water Theme Park? 

Black Thunder is a large-scale water theme and amusement park located in Mettupalayam, at the foothills of the Nilgiri Hills in Coimbatore district. Our park features 30 water rides, 29 dry rides, 23 adventure attractions, 5 dining venues, and on-site accommodation at AVANA Resort. 

Where is Black Thunder located in Coimbatore? 

Our park is situated on Ooty Main Road, Mettupalayam, approximately 40 km from Coimbatore city. The property sits at the foothills of the Nilgiri Hills, surrounded by mountain views and greenery. 

What are the opening hours of Black Thunder? 

Our park is open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. 

Is Black Thunder suitable for children? 

Yes. Our park has dedicated kids' play zones, a New Kids Pool, Kids Multi Racer slides, and child-friendly dry rides, including Santa Train, Junior Carousel, and Caterpillar Ride. Child tickets apply to visitors between 85 cm and 140 cm in height. 

Tickets and Entry at Black Thunder 

How much does an adult ticket cost at Black Thunder? 

An adult entry ticket to Black Thunder costs Rs. 1,090. This pricing is effective from 1 March 2026. 

What is the child ticket price at Black Thunder? 

Child tickets cost Rs. 850 and apply to visitors between 85 cm and 140 cm in height. Visitors exceeding 140 cm require an adult ticket upgrade at the entry gate. 

Does Black Thunder offer student discounts? 

Yes. College students pay Rs. 890 and school students pay Rs. 790. Groups of 20 or more students from the same institution receive additional concessions with a valid bona fide letter, and 1 staff member receives complimentary entry for every 15 students. 

Is there a senior citizen discount at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Senior citizens aged 60 and above pay Rs. 850 with a valid identity proof. 

Does Black Thunder offer combo ticket packages? 

Yes. A combo ticket covering park entry and adventure activities costs Rs. 1,450. Adventure activities alone cost Rs. 550. Both require a minimum height of 145 cm and a maximum weight of 85 kg. 

Water Rides at Black Thunder 

How many water rides does Black Thunder have? 

Our park features 30 water rides, ranging from high-speed slides and wave pools to family raft rides and dedicated kids' water zones. Explore all the rides here and plan your perfect day of adventure . 

Does Black Thunder have a wave pool? 

Yes. Our Wave Pool simulates ocean-like waves, making it one of the most visited attractions at the park for guests of all ages. 

Is there a lazy river at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Our Lazy River offers a slow, relaxing float through a winding water channel, suitable for visitors looking for a calmer water experience between rides. 

Does Black Thunder have water rides for young children 

Yes. Our park has a dedicated New Kids Pool, Kids Multi Racer, and Family Playstation designed for younger visitors with shallow water and age-appropriate slides. 

Does Black Thunder have a rain dance area? 

Yes. Thunder Rain is our dedicated rain dance attraction where visitors enjoy a simulated rainfall experience with music and lighting. 

Dry Rides and Attractions at Black Thunder 

How many dry rides does Black Thunder have? 

Our park features 29 dry rides, including roller coasters, spinning rides, carousels, bumper cars, and themed attractions for visitors of all ages. Explore all the rides here and discover your next adventure . 

Does Black Thunder have a roller coaster? 

Yes. Our park has 2 roller coasters: Columbus, a dragon-themed coaster winding through palm trees, and Family Coaster, an apple and caterpillar-themed ride suitable for families with children. 

Is there a 5D theatre at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Our 5D Theatre delivers an immersive cinematic experience with multi-sensory effects. A separate Cine Magic attraction is also available for cinema-based entertainment. 

Does Black Thunder have a horror house? 

Yes. Our park features 2 horror-themed attractions: Horror House, an immersive walkthrough experience, and 9D Haunted Hospital, a themed horror attraction with virtual reality elements. 

Does Black Thunder have VR experiences? 

Yes. Our park offers 2 virtual reality attractions: 9D VR, an immersive pod-based experience, and VR Play Station, an interactive gaming station with headsets and controllers. 

Are there adventure activities like zip lining at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Our adventure activities include the Zipline Coaster, Zipline Cycle, Sky Roller, Bungee Ejection, Wall Climbing, Tyre Wall Climbing, Cargo Net, and Burma Bridge, offering thrilling experiences for visitors of all ages. Explore all the adventure activities here and plan your next challenge . 

Dining at Black Thunder 

What dining options are available at Black Thunder? 

Our park offers 5 dining venues: 

Thunder Cafe (Multi-cuisine Restaurant) 

Tusker Bar (Bar) 

Bamboo Huts (Hut-themed Dining) 

BBQ & Bonfire (Outdoor Barbecue) 

Picnic By The Fountain (Outdoor Picnic Experience) 

What cuisine does Thunder Cafe at Black Thunder serve? 

Thunder Cafe serves South Indian and Chettinad cuisines in a spacious indoor dining hall with floor-to-ceiling windows and natural sunlight. A Bamboo Garden section is also available for guests who prefer outdoor seating. 
Does Black Thunder have a bar? 

Yes. Tusker Bar serves wines by the glass and bottle, cocktails, mocktails, and beers. The food menu includes fish fingers, kebabs, and Chinese entrees, served in a rustic setting inspired by traditional English clubs from tea plantation estates. 

Is there outdoor dining at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Bamboo Huts offers a traditional hut-themed dining experience with thatched-roof seating set against mountain and coconut tree views. Picnic By The Fountain provides an outdoor picnic-style dining option with seasonal fare. 

Does Black Thunder offer barbecue and bonfire dining? 

Yes. Our BBQ & Bonfire venue is an outdoor dining space at AVANA Resort, where guests enjoy barbecue meals around a bonfire on a grassy lawn setting with mountain views. 

Can visitors arrange group meals at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Custom food arrangements are available for groups, including school excursions and corporate outings. Group dining requires prior coordination with our reservations team. 

Weddings and Events at Black Thunder 

What makes Black Thunder a preferred wedding venue in Coimbatore? 

Black Thunder offers a scenic mountain backdrop, 3open-air lawns, and elegant indoor spaces, making it one of the most sought-after wedding venues in Coimbatore for a memorable celebration. 

What types of wedding venues are available at Black Thunder? 

The property features a mix of indoor and outdoor spaces , including three spacious lawns and two indoor ballrooms, suitable for both intimate ceremonies and large-scale celebrations. 
What is the guest capacity at Black Thunder wedding venues? 

Our largest lawn can accommodate up to 3,000 guests with ease, while the indoor ballrooms can host around 200 guests each, making them ideal for diverse wedding requirements. 

Does Black Thunder have outdoor wedding venues? 

Yes. Our property offers outdoor lawns, including Palm Tree Lawn (the most popular choice for weddings, surrounded by tall trees and greenery), Fountain Area (a garden venue with water fountains and seasonal flowers), and Foothill Area (a venue with mountain backdrop views). 

What is the largest wedding venue capacity at Black Thunder? 

The largest outdoor lawn at our property accommodates up to 3,000 guests. The 2 indoor ballrooms each hold approximately 200 guests. 

Does Black Thunder provide catering for events? 

Yes, Black Thunder provides beautifully designed open-air lawns surrounded by natural landscapes, perfect for couples looking for a picturesque outdoor wedding experience. 

Can external decorators and vendors be used for weddings? 

Yes, couples are welcome to bring in external decorators and vendors to customise the venue as per their wedding theme and preferences. 

Experiences and Activities at Black Thunder 

What experiences are available at Black Thunder besides rides? 

AVANA Resort offers a range of activities at Black Thunder, including bonfire evenings, cycling, sports (volleyball), animal interaction walks, fish feeding, yoga sessions, pottery making, and a Love Nest photography spot with mountain backdrops. 

Does Black Thunder offer cycling activities? 

Yes. Guests can enjoy cycling along a flat, scenic track that runs beside the sports ground for approximately half a kilometre. Suitable for both adults and children, the activity offers a refreshing ride amidst the natural surroundings. Explore all the experiences . 
Is there a bird park at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Our Bird Park features a dedicated pathway for viewing a variety of birds in their natural surroundings. An Animal Watch Tower is also available for observing wildlife across the resort grounds. 

Does Black Thunder have boating facilities? 

Yes. Our Boat House offers pedal boat rides on a calm lake surrounded by natural scenery. A separate Fish Pond is also available for fishing activities within the park. 

Can visitors interact with animals at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Nature lovers and early risers can begin their day with a 6:30 AM animal and bird sighting walk across the resort’s expansive grounds. Set amidst a natural environment, guests can observe domesticated animals such as pigeons, chickens, turtles, and rabbits, along with a variety of birds, making it a refreshing start to the day. Plan your experience here . 

Is wall climbing available at Black Thunder? 

Yes. Our park features 2 climbing attractions: a colourful artificial rock wall for standard wall climbing and a Tyre Wall, a vertical climbing structure constructed from tyres for an added challenge. 

Location & Attractions near Black Thunder 

Is Black Thunder on the route to Ooty? 

Yes. Our park is located on Ooty Main Road in Mettupalayam, directly on the highway connecting Coimbatore to Ooty, making it a convenient stop for travellers heading to the Nilgiri Hills. 

How do visitors reach Black Thunder from Coimbatore? 

Visitors travel via the Coimbatore-Mettupalayam-Ooty highway to reach our park on Ooty Main Road, Mettupalayam, approximately 40 km from Coimbatore city.

Is Black Thunder close to the Nilgiri Mountain Railway? 

Yes. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway is a scenic heritage train route passing through tunnels, forests, and hilly slopes with stops at Kellar, Coonoor, Wellington, Lovedale, and Ootacamund. Mettupalayam, where Black Thunder is located, serves as the starting point of this railway.
